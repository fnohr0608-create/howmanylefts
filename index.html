The small ones
A note on these numbers. These are estimates based on a life expectancy of 80 years. Some of us get more, some less. None of us know. That's exactly the point — not to scare you, but to remind you that the people you love, the summers you enjoy, the ordinary Mondays you take for granted: they're finite. And finite things are precious.
